Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ERRANDS NYC INC. d/b/a Errands NYC ("Errands NYC", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information, including information collected through our text messaging program.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name, email address and mobile phone number
  • Pickup and delivery addresses and order details
  • Delivery photos and proof of delivery
  • Messages you send us by text
  • Your mobile phone number and your consent to receive text messages
  • Records of your text message opt-in, opt-out and support requests
  • Location data from our Pro apps while a delivery is in progress, used for routing, tracking and proof of delivery

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Schedule, pick up and deliver orders
  • Send pickup, delivery and account notifications, including tracking links and delivery photos
  • Coordinate routes and deliveries with our delivery Pros
  • Answer your questions and provide customer support
  • Send the text messages you have agreed to receive and respond to your requests
  • Maintain records of your consent and honor opt-out requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. SMS / Text Messaging Privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent or trade your mobile number or personal information.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate our business and deliver our services, such as our text messaging platform, payment processors and hosting providers. These providers may use your information only to perform services for us and are required to protect it. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, customers or others.

5. Location Data

Temporary location history and navigation data from our Pro apps are deleted after 10 days. We keep only the GPS point of each completed delivery, for proof of delivery, customer support, dispute resolution and recordkeeping.

6. Opting Out of Text Messages

If you wish to be removed from receiving future communications, you can opt out by texting STOP, QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE. Reply HELP for assistance.

7. Data Retention, Security and Account Deletion

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law, and we protect it with encryption in transit and strict access controls. You can ask us to delete your account; we will remove all personal information not directly connected to an order.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date.
