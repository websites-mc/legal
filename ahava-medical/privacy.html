SMS Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ahava Medical and Rehabilitation LLC d/b/a Ahava Medical Center ("Ahava Medical", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information in connection with our patient text messaging program. Health information we hold as a healthcare provider is also protected under our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number
  • Your signed SMS consent, including the date it was given
  • Appointment details used in reminders (date, time, location and provider)
  • Messages you send us by text
  • Records of your opt-in, opt-out and help requests

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Send appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Send healthcare-related notifications about your visits
  • Process your replies to confirm or cancel appointments and connect you with our staff
  • Maintain records of your consent and honor opt-out requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. SMS / Text Messaging Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent or trade your mobile number or personal information.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers that help us deliver our messages and operate our practice, such as our text messaging platform, and only as needed to provide those services. These providers may use your information only to perform services for us and are required to protect it. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Opting Out of Text Messages

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP or 4 to any message. You may also reply END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE or QUIT. Reply HELP for help or 3 to speak with staff.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep your mobile number and consent records while you are an active patient or until you opt out, and we keep your opt-out status so we can honor it. We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information.

7. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your text messaging information by contacting us using the details below, subject to medical record retention requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new effective date.
