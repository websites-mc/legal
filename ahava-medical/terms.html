2. How to Opt In

You can join the Program by completing and signing the SMS Text Messaging Consent form at any Ahava Medical location and writing the mobile number at which you want to receive messages. Consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not required to receive medical services.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your appointment schedule.

Message and data rates may apply. Please check your mobile plan or contact your wireless carrier for details.

5. How to Opt Out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP or 4 to any message to unsubscribe. You may also reply END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE or QUIT. To cancel an appointment without unsubscribing, reply 2. After you opt out, you will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further messages from Ahava Medical Center unless you opt in again.
